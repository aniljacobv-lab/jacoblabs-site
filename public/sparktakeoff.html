SparkTakeoff

AI-assisted electrical estimating. Upload a plan set, auto-detect components, and price the job against NEC 2023 — right in the browser.

Open the app ↗

The problem

Electrical takeoffs are slow, manual, and error-prone — counting symbols across dozens of plan sheets, then pricing each one by hand. SparkTakeoff compresses that into an upload-and-review workflow.

How it works

Drop in a plan set and the app detects components automatically, then prices the job against NEC 2023 requirements. Everything runs in the browser as a PWA, so estimators can use it on-site without installing anything.

What it does

  • Upload a plan set and auto-detect electrical components
  • Price the job against NEC 2023 codes
  • Runs in the browser as an installable PWA
  • Built for estimators in the field, not just the office
