Deck Forge

Describe the deck you need, pick how deep to go, and generate a sourced presentation — routed across multiple AI models for the best result.

Open the app ↗

The idea

Building a solid deck means research, structure, and writing — usually across several tools. Deck Forge collapses that into a prompt: describe what you need and how deep to go.

How it works

Requests are routed across multiple AI models, each used where it is strongest, and the output is a sourced presentation rather than unverified filler — so you start from something defensible.

What it does

  • Generate a full presentation from a short description
  • Choose how deep the research and detail should go
  • Multi-model routing — the right model for each step
  • Sourced output you can actually stand behind
